# LUDO GLOBAL — AGENT RULES

## Core Workflow Rules
1. **Explain in Telugu (Step 1):** Before implementation, explain what was understood, what is planned, how it will work, and compliance impact.
2. **Wait for Approval (Step 2):** Do not start implementation until user explicitly approves.
3. **Implementation & Testing (Step 3 & 4):** Implement cleanly, run tests, verify zero errors/warnings.
4. **5-Line Response Rule:** Once a task/verification is complete, the final response MUST be concise and strictly within 5 lines in Telugu.

## Legal & Compliance Principles
- Zero real-money gambling, betting, cash prizes, or monetary transfers.
- Strict compliance with Google Play, Apple App Store, and Indian Online Gaming Act 2025.
- All purchases/rewards are strictly cosmetic virtual goods.
